SAY WHAT?!

Official Privacy Policy | Jolly Bear Studios

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Jolly Bear Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops SAY WHAT?!, an entertainment application that transforms user-submitted text into humorous, expressive dialogue and may provide generated character voices.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, stored, and shared when users interact with SAY WHAT?!.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, SAY WHAT?! may process:

  • Firebase account identifiers and authentication information.
  • Text entered for translation or comedy generation.
  • Selected characters, languages, styles, and other preferences.
  • Generated translations, associated results, and audio.
  • Translation balances, reward eligibility, and usage records.
  • Subscription and purchase-related identifiers and entitlements.
  • Advertising reward verification information.
  • Technical information needed for security, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, and service operation.

Some information may be stored locally on the user's device, while account and transaction-related information may be stored on our backend systems.

3. How Information Is Used

We process information to:

  • Authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • Generate humorous translations and character dialogue.
  • Generate or replay supported voice content.
  • Maintain translation credits and account entitlements.
  • Validate advertising rewards and prevent fraudulent claims.
  • Verify purchases and manage subscription benefits.
  • Maintain service security, reliability, and functionality.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

4. Artificial Intelligence Services

SAY WHAT?! uses AI-powered services to create entertainment content. Text submitted for supported translation features may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing.

Where character voice generation is available, relevant text may also be transmitted to ElevenLabs to generate speech audio.

These providers process information under their respective privacy policies and applicable service terms.

Users should avoid entering passwords, payment card numbers, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into translation fields.

5. Authentication and Account Information

SAY WHAT?! uses Google Firebase Authentication to support account access and identify authenticated users.

Account identifiers may be associated with translation balances, saved results, promotional entitlements, purchase information, and security records.

Firebase-related information is processed according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

6. Advertising

SAY WHAT?! may offer Google AdMob rewarded advertisements that allow eligible users to earn additional translation opportunities.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, and other information as described in Google's policies.

Reward verification information is processed to ensure that translation credits are awarded only after successful verification.

Advertising availability and eligibility may vary.

7. Purchases and Subscriptions

SAY WHAT?! may offer paid translation credits, subscriptions, and premium entertainment features.

Where purchases are available through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We may receive purchase tokens, product identifiers, transaction status, and subscription entitlement information necessary to verify and provide purchases.

We do not require users to enter their full payment card details into SAY WHAT?! for Google Play purchases.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Our backend may maintain account identifiers, translation results, usage records, purchase-related information, and advertising reward records.

Information is retained as necessary for providing the service, maintaining account entitlements, preventing abuse, addressing disputes, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the category of information and the purpose for which it is held. We do not currently specify a single automatic deletion period applicable to all stored information.

9. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary for application functionality, including:

  • Google Firebase - authentication and related services.
  • OpenAI - AI text processing.
  • ElevenLabs - supported speech generation.
  • Google AdMob - advertising and reward verification.
  • Google Play - purchases and subscription services.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers - application hosting and backend data storage.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

10. Data Security

We use measures intended to protect account information and application services, including authenticated API access, HTTPS communication, and server-side validation of supported reward and purchase operations.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Users may contact us to request information about personal data associated with their account, request correction where applicable, or request account and associated data deletion.

Requests are reviewed subject to identity verification, technical capabilities, and applicable legal or transaction-record retention requirements.

13. Children's Privacy

We recognize the importance of protecting children's privacy. Any availability of SAY WHAT?! to younger users is subject to applicable age requirements, parental consent requirements where relevant, and Google Play policies.

Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns or information relating to a child.

14. International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence. Such processing is subject to applicable data protection requirements and the providers' respective practices.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SAY WHAT?! introduces or changes features, data processing practices, service providers, or legal requirements.

The current version will be available at this page, with its updated effective date.
